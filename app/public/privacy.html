Pocket Hotel: Privacy policy

Open the app

Pocket Hotel is a hotel app for small hotels, lodges and guest houses in Nepal. It works without internet and saves data on the device. When signed in, it also keeps a copy on a server so several phones can share it.

What is stored

  • The hotel's details: name, address, PAN/VAT number, phone, rooms and prices.
  • Bookings, bills, payments, restaurant orders and cleaning status.
  • Guest details the hotel types in: name, phone, nationality, ID or passport number and visa details. ID photos are off by default.
  • Staff names, login names and PIN codes. PINs and passwords are stored only as salted hashes, never in plain text.
  • The owner's email address, used to sign in and to send a 6-digit code.
  • A record of important actions (cancelled bookings, price changes, staff changes) and which devices signed in.

Who can see it

Each hotel can only see its own data. Inside a hotel, the owner decides what each staff member can see and do. The person who runs the Pocket Hotel service can reach the server database for support and security work. We do not sell data, show ads or share data with advertisers.

Where it is kept

Data is kept on this device and, for signed-in hotels, on Cloudflare's servers (Cloudflare Workers and D1 database). It is encrypted in transit. It is not end-to-end encrypted, so the server operator is technically able to read it.

Email

The service sends sign-in codes and account messages by email through a Gmail account that belongs to the service operator. This uses only the permission to send mail. It cannot read anyone's mailbox. Pocket Hotel does not ask for access to your Google account.

Keeping and deleting data

A hotel owner can ask to close the account in the app (Settings, Account). After the request is approved, the hotel's data on the server is erased 14 days later. Data already stored on a phone or computer stays there until it is cleared on that device. Hotels are responsible for asking guests for permission before storing their ID details, and for keeping them only as long as needed.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/king55-hack/pocket-hotel/issues.
